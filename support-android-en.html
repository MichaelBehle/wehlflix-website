What support can help with

Support can help with incorrect local times, incomplete fight cards, premium questions, restoring Google Play purchases, reminder issues, and technical problems with the Android app.

Premium on Android is a one-time Google Play purchase, not a subscription. If it is not recognized, open the app with the same Google account used for the purchase and choose More → Restore Purchases.

No Spoiler MMA loads current event and fight data through the Wehlflix edge API. This API may use providers such as BALLDONTLIE MMA server-side. UFC coverage is currently the strongest, while coverage of other promotions may be partial or delayed.

Ads, reminders, and permissions

If ads are active in your region, the Android app may display a Google consent dialog at launch when legally required.

The app requests notification permission only when you explicitly enable local reminders for an event.

Reminder notifications remain on your Android device. No push notifications are sent remotely from my servers.
