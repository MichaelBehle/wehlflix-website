Android Support

Support, contact details, and legal information for the Android version of No Spoiler MMA.

Contact

Michael Wehle

Email: MichaelWehle@proton.me

Support is handled by email.

What support can help with

Support can help with incorrect local times, incomplete fight cards, premium questions, restoring Google Play purchases, reminder issues, and technical problems with the Android app.

Premium on Android is a one-time Google Play purchase, not a subscription. If it is not recognized, open the app with the same Google account used for the purchase and choose More → Restore Purchases.

No Spoiler MMA uses BALLDONTLIE MMA for current event and fight data. UFC coverage is currently the strongest, while other promotions may be partial or delayed.

Ads, reminders, and permissions

If ads are active in your region, the Android app may display a Google consent flow when legally required.

The app requests notification permission only when you enable a local reminder for an event.

Reminder notifications remain on your Android device. No remote push notifications are sent from my servers.
