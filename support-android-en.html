Android Support

This page contains support, contact details, and legal information for the Android version of No Spoiler MMA.

Contact

Michael Wehle

Email: MichaelWehle@proton.me

Support is handled by email.

What support can help with

Support can help with the following issues: incorrect local times, incomplete fight cards, premium questions, restoring Google Play purchases, reminder issues, and technical problems with the Android app.

Premium on Android is a one-time Google Play purchase, not a subscription. If it is not recognized, open the app with the same Google account used for the purchase and select More → Restore Purchases.

No Spoiler MMA uses BallDon'tLie MMA for current event and fight data. The UFC currently has the strongest coverage, while coverage of other promotions may be partial or delayed.

Ads, reminders, and permissions

If ads are active in your region, the Android app may display a Google consent flow upon launch, if legally required.

The app only requests permission to send notifications when you enable local reminders for an event.

Reminder notifications remain on your Android device. No push notifications are sent remotely from my servers.
