Android Support

Support and legal information for the Android version of NoSpoilerMMA.

Contact

Michael Wehle

Email: MichaelWehle@proton.me

Support is handled by email.

What support can help with

Support can help with incorrect local times, incomplete fight cards, premium questions, Google Play purchase restoration, reminder issues, and technical problems inside the Android app.

Premium on Android is a one-time Google Play purchase, not a subscription. If Premium is not recognized, please use More -> Restore purchases on the same Google account that completed the purchase.

NoSpoilerMMA uses BALLDONTLIE MMA for live MMA event and fight data. UFC currently has the strongest coverage; other promotions can be partial or delayed.

Ads, reminders, and permissions

If ads are active in your region, the Android app may show a Google consent flow at launch when legally required.

The app only asks for notification permission when you actively enable local reminders for an event.

Reminder notifications are local to your Android device. No remote push service is operated by me.
