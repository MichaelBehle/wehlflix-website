Android Privacy Policy

Privacy information for the Android version of No Spoiler MMA.

Controller

Michael Wehle

Email: MichaelWehle@proton.me

What the Android app stores locally

Favorites, spoiler settings, reveal state, reminder preferences, time zone settings, and Premium status are stored locally on your Android device.

I do not operate my own user account system, and I do not run my own backend for this local app state.

Event and fight data

To load current MMA event and fight-card data, the Android app connects directly to BALLDONTLIE MMA. Technical connection data such as your IP address and HTTP metadata may therefore be processed by that provider.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Google Play purchases

Premium on Android is offered as a one-time purchase through Google Play Billing. If you purchase or restore Premium, the transaction is handled through Google Play. I do not receive your credit card information.

Google processes account and transaction data required for the purchase flow. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Notifications and reminders

The Android app requests notification permission only when you enable a reminder. Reminder data is stored locally on the device and used only for local notifications.

No remote push notifications are sent from my own servers.

Ads and consent

If ads are active, the Android app uses Google advertising services and may use Google's consent flow when legally required for your region.

Depending on the configuration and your region, Google may process your IP address, device information, consent state, ad requests, and ad interaction data.

More information: Google Privacy Policy.

Support requests

If you contact me by email, I process your message, email address, timestamp, and any information you provide voluntarily in order to answer your request.

Your rights

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, and complaint under applicable privacy law.

The current Android policy is always available at wehlflix.com/privacy-android.html.
