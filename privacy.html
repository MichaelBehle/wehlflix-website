4. Data Processing in the App

App Store Download

When downloading the app from the Apple App Store, data (e.g. Apple ID, download time, device info) is transmitted to Apple Inc. I have no influence over this. Please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy.

Premium Purchases & Restore

Premium is offered as a one-time purchase without a subscription. If you buy Premium or restore a previous purchase, the transaction is handled directly through Apple StoreKit and the Apple App Store. Apple processes your Apple ID together with transaction and purchase information for that flow. I do not receive separate payment details such as credit card information. Restore uses the same Apple ID that was originally used for the purchase. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Local Data Storage

Settings and local content saved by you, such as spoiler preferences, favorites, reminder state, and custom fight cards, are stored on your device. These locally stored items are not transmitted to my servers. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

No User Accounts

The app does not require registration or account creation. The app itself does not transmit personal data to my own servers.

Support Requests by Email

If you contact me by email, I process your message, sender address, timestamps, and any content or attachments you include voluntarily, such as the event name, timezone, device details, or screenshots. This data is used solely to handle the request. Email transmission and storage also technically involve the email providers used by the sender and recipient. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for app-related support requests and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR for other contact requests. The data is deleted once the request has been completed unless statutory retention obligations apply.

External Event Data Requests

When current event and fight card data is loaded, the app connects directly to external sources such as ESPN and TheSportsDB. Technical connection data such as your IP address and device-related HTTP metadata may be processed by those providers. I do not directly control that processing. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Time Zone & World Time Feature

The app reads the device's time zone automatically from iOS (TimeZone.current) to display world times (e.g. US time zones). This process takes place entirely on your device. No location data is requested and no personal data is collected or transmitted. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Event Reminders

You can enable local iOS notifications for existing events. Reminder-related information is stored on your device. No remote push notifications are sent from my servers. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR.

Calendar Export

If you choose to save an event to your calendar, the app requests calendar access only when that feature is used. The app uses that access only to store the event entry you selected on your device, and the entry is written directly to your Apple calendar. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR.

5. App Permissions

The app requests the following device access:

Permission Purpose
Local iOS Notifications Local iOS notifications for event reminders. Consent granted via the iOS system prompt. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR.
Calendar Saving selected events to your calendar after explicit permission. Access is only used for the calendar entry you trigger. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR.
App Tracking Transparency (ATT) The current version does not request the iOS tracking permission automatically. If a later version introduces personalized advertising with an ATT prompt, this privacy policy will be updated before that release ships.

You can disable local reminder notifications at any time under Settings → Notifications → SpoilerFree MMA.

External Event Data Sources

For current event and fight card data, the app primarily uses ESPN and TheSportsDB. When your device requests this data, technical connection data may be processed directly by those providers. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

If ads are active, the app uses Google's consent SDK UMP together with the advertising service Google AdMob provided by:

Based on the app's current functionality, neither the app nor the integrated SDKs processes special categories of personal data such as health data, precise location, financial information, political opinions, religious beliefs, or biometric data.

7. Data Retention

All data stored locally on your device, such as settings, favorites, reminder state, and custom fight cards, is retained until you change it, remove it, or uninstall the app. No data is stored on external servers by me.

Data processed by Google AdMob (e.g. IDFA-based advertising data) is retained by Google Ireland Limited according to their own retention policy. For details, see Google's data retention information.

9. Children's Privacy

SpoilerFree MMA is not directed at children under the age of 13 (COPPA) or 16 (GDPR). I do not knowingly collect personal data from children under these ages.

If I become aware that a child under 13 has provided personal data, I will delete it immediately. Parents or guardians may contact MichaelWehle@proton.me for such requests.

10. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) grants you specific rights, including the right to know what personal data is collected, the right to deletion, and the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal data.

SpoilerFree MMA does not sell personal data. The use of advertising identifiers by Google AdMob may qualify as "sharing" under CPRA. You can manage ad choices as follows:

  • If available: More → Ads & Privacy in the app
  • iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking

To exercise any other CCPA/CPRA rights, contact: MichaelWehle@proton.me
