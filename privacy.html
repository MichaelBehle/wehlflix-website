4. Data Processing in the App

App Store Download

When downloading the app from the Apple App Store, data (e.g. Apple ID, download time, device info) is transmitted to Apple Inc. I have no influence over this. Please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy.

Local Data Storage

All user data (settings, events) is stored exclusively on your device. This data does not leave your device and is not transmitted to external servers. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

No User Accounts

The app does not require registration or account creation. No personal data is transmitted to my servers.

Time Zone & World Time Feature

The app reads the device's time zone automatically from iOS (TimeZone.current) to display world times (e.g. US time zones). This process takes place entirely on your device. No location data is requested and no personal data is collected or transmitted. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Event Reminders

You can create events in the app for which you will be reminded via push notification. Event data (title, date, time) is stored locally on your device only. To deliver the reminder, the Apple Push Notification Service (APNs) is used (see Section 6). Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR.

5. App Permissions

The app requests the following device access:

Permission Purpose
Push Notifications Sending event reminders for upcoming appointments. Consent granted via iOS system prompt. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR.
App Tracking Transparency (ATT) Before using your Advertising Identifier (IDFA) for personalized ads, the app displays an iOS system prompt (App Tracking Transparency framework, ATT). 6. Third Parties & SDKs

Apple Push Notification Service (APNs)

To deliver push notifications, the app uses the Apple Push Notification Service (APNs) by Apple Inc. A device-specific push token is transmitted to Apple. This token does not directly identify you by name, but constitutes pseudonymous personal data under the GDPR, as it uniquely identifies your device. For more information, see Apple's Privacy Policy. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR.

In-App Advertising – Google AdMob

This app uses the advertising service Google AdMob provided by:

Google Ireland Limited

Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland

AdMob uses advertising identifiers (e.g. Advertising ID / IDFA) to serve ads and measure their effectiveness. Data such as your IP address, device information, and interactions with ads are transmitted to and processed by Google.

Purpose: Displaying personalized or contextual advertisements and analyzing app usage.
Legal basis: Processing is based on your explicit consent pursuant to Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, which you can grant or withdraw via the consent banner (CMP) displayed in the app.
Opt-out: You can withdraw your consent and restrict personalized ads at any time: iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track".

Data categories collected by AdMob:

  • Advertising Identifier (IDFA) – only with ATT consent
  • IP address (used to derive coarse location)
  • Device information (OS version, device model, language)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, view duration)
  • App usage data (session length, in-app events)

Google Ireland Limited may transfer data to Google LLC servers in the USA. Google LLC is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (DPF), ensuring an adequate level of data protection. Legal basis for third-country transfer: Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR (Standard Contractual Clauses).

More information: Google Privacy Policy (policies.google.com)

Withdraw consent: You can withdraw or change your advertising consent at any time via Settings → Privacy within the app (if a CMP settings option is available) or via iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Neither the app nor any integrated SDK collects sensitive categories of personal data such as health data, precise location, financial information, political opinions, religious beliefs, or biometric data.

7. Data Retention

All data stored locally on your device (events, settings) is retained until you uninstall the app or manually delete the data within the app. No data is stored on external servers by me.

Data processed by Google AdMob (e.g. IDFA-based advertising data) is retained by Google Ireland Limited according to their own retention policy. For details, see Google's data retention information.

SpoilerFreeMMA is not directed at children under the age of 13 (COPPA) or 16 (GDPR). I do not knowingly collect personal data from children under these ages.

If I become aware that a child under 13 has provided personal data, I will delete it immediately. Parents or guardians may contact MichaelWehle@proton.me for such requests.

10. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) grants you specific rights, including the right to know what personal data is collected, the right to deletion, and the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal data.

SpoilerFreeMMA does not sell personal data. The use of advertising identifiers (IDFA) by Google AdMob for cross-context behavioral advertising may qualify as "sharing" under CPRA. You can opt out at any time:

  • iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track"
  • iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising → disable "Personalized Ads"

To exercise any other CCPA/CPRA rights, contact: MichaelWehle@proton.me
