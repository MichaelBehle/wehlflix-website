What support can help with

Support can help with incorrect times, incomplete fight cards, premium questions, purchase restoration, and technical issues in the app.

Premium is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. If Premium is not recognized on your device, please first use More → Premium → Restore purchases in the app with the same Apple ID that was originally used for the purchase.

For faster support, please include the event name, your selected time zone, the affected device, and a screenshot.

Ads & privacy notes

If ad consent options are available for your region, you can open them in the app under More → Ads & privacy.

You can review system-wide tracking settings in iOS under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Detailed privacy information is available at privacy.html.
