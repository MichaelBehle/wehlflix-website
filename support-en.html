What support can help with

Support can help with incorrect event times, incomplete fight cards, Premium issues, purchase restoration, and technical problems in the app.

Premium is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. If it is not recognized on your device, first use More → Restore Purchases in the app with the same Apple ID that was originally used for the purchase.

The app supports UFC, ONE Championship, PFL, and Bellator whenever the underlying event data is available.

To help resolve issues faster, please include the event name, your selected time zone, the affected device, and a screenshot.

Ads and Privacy

If ad consent options are available in your region, you can access them in the app by going to More → Ads & Privacy.

When ads are active, the app checks at launch whether a consent step is required for your region.

The current version only shows the required Google consent flows. It does not automatically display a separate iOS tracking prompt.

The full privacy policy also explains which data categories may be processed when ads are active, including identifiers, coarse location, diagnostics, advertising data, and product interaction.

Detailed privacy information is available at privacy.html.
